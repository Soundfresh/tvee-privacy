Privacy Policy

TVee Remote ("Application") respects the privacy of its users.

1. Data Collection

The Application does not collect or store any personal data such as name, email, or location.

2. Permissions Usage

  • Local Network Access – used only to discover and connect to TVs in your Wi-Fi network.
  • Microphone – used only to send voice commands to your TV.
  • Vibration – used only to provide haptic feedback during remote control actions.

These permissions are used strictly for functionality and no data is sent to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application uses third-party services to manage subscriptions and purchases (e.g., RevenueCat). These services may process anonymized purchase and device-related data necessary for subscription functionality.

The Application does not use advertising services or track users for marketing purposes.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

The Application offers optional premium features via subscriptions and one-time purchases:

  • Weekly Subscription – includes a 3-day free trial and automatically renews.
  • Yearly Subscription – automatically renews.
  • Lifetime Purchase – one-time payment, no renewal.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment will be charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account at confirmation of purchase.

Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. Prices may vary depending on your region.

You can manage or cancel your subscriptions at any time in your account settings:

  • Apple devices: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions
  • Android devices: Google Play → Payments & Subscriptions

Free trial (if available) will automatically convert to a paid subscription unless canceled before the trial period ends.

We do not collect or store any payment information. All payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play.

5. Changes

If new features are added (for example, subscriptions or analytics), this Privacy Policy will be updated.
